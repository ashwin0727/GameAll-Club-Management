Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This explains what information GameAll collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We have kept it plain.

Who we are and what this covers

GameAll is the app (and website, play.gameall.co) a player uses to find sports venues, book courts, join memberships and coaching, and pay. This policy covers GameAll. Venues use a separate app, GameAll Venue, which has its own policy.

Contact us at admin@gameall.co or 9360428761. Website: gameall.co. If you have a concern about your data, write to the same address and we will reply within 7 days.

What we collect

  • Your account: your mobile number (it is your sign-in, verified with a one-time code by SMS), your name and, if you choose to give it, your email address.
  • What you do in the app: the bookings you make, memberships and coaching programmes you join, their schedules and attendance, and the payments and refunds that go with them.
  • Payments: amounts, status, method and Razorpay reference numbers. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure screen and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Location (only if you allow it): the app can use your phone's approximate location to pick your city and show the nearest venues first. The position is used on your phone to sort the list. It is not sent to us or stored.
  • Notifications: a notification token for your phone, so we can tell you about bookings, payments and reminders.
  • Technical information: basic device and crash information so we can keep the app working and fix faults.

How we use it

  • To run the service you asked for: bookings, memberships and coaching.
  • To take and record payments and to refund them under the Cancellation and Refund Policy.
  • To send notifications such as upcoming bookings, payment results and reminders. You can switch categories off in the app.
  • To verify accounts, prevent misuse and keep the service secure.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

What a venue can see about you

When you book, join a membership or enrol in coaching at a venue, that venue sees your name, mobile number, the booking or enrolment and what you paid, so it can serve you. The venue is responsible for how it uses that information. Other venues cannot see it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising. We use these providers to run the service, and they handle data only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Razorpay: online payments, refunds and Auto Pay mandates.
  • Google Firebase: push notifications and crash reports.
  • 2Factor: one-time codes by SMS.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data. We keep payment and tax records for as long as the law requires, without the details that identify you where possible. A venue's own booking and attendance records about its customers stay with the venue.

Deleting your account and data

In GameAll open Profile, then Support & Legal, then Delete my account. You can also ask from any browser on the account deletion page. Deleting removes your sign-in and personal data as described above, and cancels any Auto Pay so nobody is charged again.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each venue's data is restricted to its owner and the staff the owner has authorised, and this is enforced in the database itself. The app does not copy your sign-in to cloud backups. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep the codes we send you private.

Children

GameAll is for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your details in Profile, switch notifications off in the app, withdraw location access in your phone's settings at any time, and ask us to correct or delete your data using the contact above. Under Indian data-protection law you may also ask what we hold about you and complain to us about how it is handled.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
