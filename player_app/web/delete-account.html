Delete your account

Last updated 15 November 2026

You can delete your GameAll account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Here is how.

From the app (quickest)

  1. Open GameAll and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Support & Legal.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email admin@gameall.co from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number on it. We confirm it is you, then delete the account and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, your name, email and phone on your profile, and your notification settings.
  • Any Auto Pay you set up. It is cancelled so nobody is charged again.
  • A venue keeps its own booking, attendance and payment records of you, as a business must, without your GameAll profile attached.

What we keep

Only one thing delays a deletion: online payments that have not yet been settled. We keep those payment records and close your account straight away, and everything else is deleted automatically once they are settled. See the Privacy Policy for more.
