Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

How cancelling works in GameAll Play, and how and when refunds are returned. It is the same schedule the app shows you before you confirm.

Court bookings you have paid in full

You can cancel a court booking in GameAll Play under My bookings. Before you confirm, the app shows exactly what you will get back. The amount kept depends on how long is left before the booking starts:

Bookings where only the advance is paid

A court booking longer than an hour can be secured with a 50% advance. If you cancel a booking that is only part-paid, the advance is not refunded, unless the venue chooses to refund it. Pay in full if you want the schedule above to apply. A booking you never paid for can be cancelled at no cost.

How refunds are returned

  • Money you paid online goes back to the same UPI app, card or bank account it came from, through Razorpay. Your bank usually credits it within 5 to 7 working days.
  • If part of the booking was paid in cash at the venue, that part is refunded by the venue, and the app tells you so.
  • If the venue cancels or cannot provide the court, the venue refunds what you paid. If that does not happen, write to us and we will follow it up.

Memberships and coaching

  • Each venue sets its own membership and coaching terms, shown on the plan or programme before you pay. Fees for a period already started are normally not refundable.
  • If you joined by Auto Pay you can stop future charges at any time from your UPI app or from GameAll Play; nothing is charged after you cancel. A charge already made is not reversed automatically.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or twice, write to us and we will sort it out with the venue and Razorpay.

If something went wrong

If money left your account but your booking or membership did not appear, do not pay again. It is normally settled automatically within a few minutes; if it is not, write to admin@gameall.co with your phone number and the time of payment and we will fix it or refund you.
