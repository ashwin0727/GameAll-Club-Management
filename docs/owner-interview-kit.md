# Court owner interview kit

Goal: talk to 8–10 badminton and pickleball court owners and find out what they would pay for GameAll Club Management.
Plans being tested: **Basic ₹599/month** and **Advanced ₹1,499/month** (yearly: ₹5,999 and ₹14,999).

## 1. First message (WhatsApp or Instagram)

> Hi, I'm [your name] from GameAll. We make software that helps badminton and pickleball courts run bookings, memberships and payments. Could I ask you 3 quick questions about how you run your court? It takes about 10 minutes and I'm not selling anything. Happy to give you a free month of the app as a thank you.

## 2. If they don't reply (after 2 days)

> Hi [name], just checking in. Even a 5-minute call would help us a lot. When is a good time?

## 3. Walk-in opener (in person)

> Hi, I'm [your name]. I'm building software for court owners and want to learn how you run things. Do you have 10 minutes? I'm not here to sell, I only want your honest advice.

## 4. Questions (tick or write the answer)

**About the court**
1. How many courts do you have, and which sports?
2. Roughly how many bookings a day on a normal day?
3. Do you run memberships or coaching as well?

**How they work today**
4. How do you take bookings now? (notebook / WhatsApp / spreadsheet / another app)
5. How do you collect payment? (cash / UPI / bank transfer / online)
6. What is the most painful part of running the court?
7. Do you pay for any software today? Which one and how much per month?

**Price (read the features first, then ask)**

Basic: bookings, calendar, guest booking, memberships and coaching with manual payment tracking, reports, tournaments, next-day payouts.
Advanced: everything in Basic plus Auto Pay collection, finance (daily closing, profit and loss), staff roles, maintenance, guest player lists, players can enrol in memberships from the app, payouts within 5 minutes.

8. Would ₹599 a month for Basic feel: cheap / fair / expensive?
9. Would ₹1,499 a month for Advanced feel: cheap / fair / expensive?
10. At what monthly price would it feel **too cheap to trust**?
11. At what monthly price would you **not even consider it**?
12. Which one feature would make you pay for Advanced?
13. Would you prefer to pay monthly or yearly (two months free)?

## 6. How to read the results

- If most say ₹599 is **cheap or fair**, keep it. If most say **expensive**, drop Basic towards ₹399–499.
- If most say ₹1,499 is **fair**, keep it. If most say **expensive** but answer 12 names a clear feature, keep the price and market that feature.
- The "too cheap to trust" answer sets the floor: do not price below it.
- Look at courts-per-owner. If larger venues accept a higher price, consider a price per court later.
